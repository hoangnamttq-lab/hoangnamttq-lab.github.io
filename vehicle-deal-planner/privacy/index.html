Your records and your choices

Vehicle Deal Planner is provided by Nam Tran. This policy describes the iOS app and these information pages. For questions or a privacy request, email hoangnamttq@gmail.com.

Vehicle records are stored on your device. The app does not automatically upload your vehicle notes, photos, buyer details, repair records or deal amounts to the developer or Firebase.

No app account is required. There are no advertising SDKs or AI services processing your records. We do not sell your information or use it for targeted advertising.

Information stored on your device

Your workspace contains vehicle names, buying plans, baseline snapshots, costs, quotes, bills, payments, sale details, notes, attachments and history. Preferences, drafts, report branding, deleted records available for undo, and the record of your Free allowance are also stored locally.

The developer cannot open or recover this local workspace remotely. iOS device backups and transfers may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Subscriptions through Apple

Apple processes payment and purchase transactions. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit 2 framework to retrieve available products and prices, verify subscription access, restore purchases and open subscription management. Apple-signed transaction and subscription information is checked on your device to determine Pro access. The app does not use a separate subscription-management provider.

Your vehicle workspace is not sent with these purchase requests. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple handles App Store account and transaction information under Apple’s privacy information.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

Some builds offer an optional analytics and diagnostics switch in Settings. It starts off. If the switch is available and you enable it, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics process app activity and technical diagnostics to help improve the app.

Activity includes screens visited and whether actions such as creating, deleting, exporting, restoring or purchasing succeeded. Sent values are restricted to predefined categories, settings and product identifiers; they do not include vehicle names, notes, photos, buyer details or vehicle financial amounts. Diagnostics can include installation identifiers, app/iOS versions and crash traces.

Turning this choice off stops future optional collection and resets the app’s analytics data on the device; it does not automatically delete records already processed by the service. When this choice is not offered, the app does not enable Firebase collection. More information: Firebase privacy and security.

Photos, exports and backups

Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. Selected or captured images are copied into local app storage. You control camera and photo access through iOS.

Reports and backup ZIPs are created locally. The system share sheet opens only when you choose to share; the destination you select then receives the file. Backups contain business records and photos and are not password-protected by the app. A report can contain identifying or financial information, depending on its contents and mode. Review the file before sharing and use a destination you trust.

Retention, deletion and requests

Local information remains in app storage until removed. Deleting a vehicle moves it to the app’s local deleted-records list so it can be restored; this is not permanent erasure. Deleting or archiving records does not reset the saved Free allowance. Removing the app can remove its local workspace, but does not remove backups, exported copies, support emails or Apple transaction records.

We use purchase-service information to operate subscriptions and resolve related issues, and optional diagnostics to identify usage or reliability problems. Retention is limited by those purposes, provider settings and applicable recordkeeping requirements. We do not promise that previously processed data disappears when a device setting changes.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of information handled by us. Contact support with enough information to identify the request; never send your password. We can help with records under our control and relevant service-provider requests, but cannot read or remotely erase your local workspace. Some transaction or legal records may need to be retained. Providers may process service data outside your country.

Support and younger users

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever details or files you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve the request; app records are not automatically attached. Ask us to delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

The app is intended for people managing vehicle deals and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to support.

This website and updates

These pages use no developer analytics, advertising scripts, account system or contact form. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We update this page when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies this version. Contact support if you need clarification about the version you use.
