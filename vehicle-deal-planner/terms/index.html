What the app does

The app organizes your buying plans, costs, payments and recorded sale outcomes. Its calculations depend on information you enter. Forecasts are estimates; unknown amounts are not guarantees of zero cost. The app does not inspect a vehicle, appraise its market value, verify a buyer or seller, or guarantee a profit.

Review the underlying records before making a decision or sharing a report. You remain responsible for your transactions, taxes and any licensing or disclosure obligations that apply to your activities.

Free and Pro access

Free access lets you start one real vehicle record in the local workspace and continue managing that record. Deleting, archiving or restoring data does not refresh this allowance. Sample data is separate from real records.

With verified active Pro access, you can add more vehicles, compare your portfolio and create batch or branded reports. If Pro expires, your existing vehicle records remain available for editing and basic export, including recording later costs. Features that require Pro may no longer be available; expiry does not delete your workspace.

Monthly and Yearly subscriptions

The Pro store displays available Monthly and Yearly plans, their current localized prices and billing periods. Review the full charge and renewal information shown in the app and Apple’s purchase confirmation before approving a purchase. An illustration or screenshot is not a price offer.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled. Cancel anytime using Manage subscription in the app or your Apple Account’s Subscriptions settings. Turning off renewal normally leaves access active until the current paid period ends, subject to Apple’s transaction status, refunds and revocations. Cancellation does not itself request a refund.

For Apple’s current instructions, see cancel a subscription. For refund requests, use Apple’s Report a Problem service; Apple determines eligibility.

Restoring access and protecting your data

Restore purchases checks previous purchases associated with your Apple Account; it does not make a new purchase. Subscription restoration is different from restoring vehicle data. There is no automatic developer cloud copy of your vehicle workspace.

Create and save a backup before reinstalling the app, replacing a workspace or moving to another device. Exported reports and backup ZIPs can contain business and personal information. Review the contents and choose the recipient or storage destination deliberately. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Availability and support

Local recordkeeping works without a continuous Internet connection. Product loading, purchases, entitlement refresh and purchase restoration require access to the relevant Apple and RevenueCat services. A pending or unverified transaction does not establish verified Pro access.
