Contact support

Email hoangnamttq@gmail.com. You can also use Contact support or Copy email in the app’s Settings.

Include the app version/build, iOS version, what you expected and the steps that caused the issue. A screenshot is helpful if it contains no information you want to keep private. Records and attachments are never automatically sent with a support request. Do not send Apple passwords, verification codes or payment-card details.

Pro purchase or restoration

  1. Connect to the Internet and open the Pro store. If plans cannot load, use Try again. Existing vehicle records remain available.
  2. If you already purchased Pro, use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Do not buy again merely because access has not refreshed.
  3. Restore can report an active purchase, no active purchase or an error. If it remains unavailable, send support the app build and the message shown. Do not send your full receipt unless support specifically explains why it is needed.

Use Manage subscription in Settings to review renewal with Apple. You can also open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple’s cancellation instructions and Apple’s refund service.

Cancel anytime. Turning off renewal usually preserves access through the paid period; deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. The app cannot approve refunds or change your Apple Account.

Back up or move your workspace

  1. Open Settings → Create backup.
  2. When the ZIP is ready, use Share and save it to Files or another location you trust. Creating the file inside the app alone does not put a copy outside the app.
  3. Check that the saved ZIP is available before removing the app or changing devices.

A backup includes workspace records, photos and usage history. It is not password-protected by the app. Restoring a subscription does not restore vehicle data.

To import a backup, choose Settings → Restore vehicle data, select the ZIP, and review its vehicle count, attachment count and currency. Choose Merge without overwriting or Replace workspace. Merge refuses conflicting versions of the same record; replacement uses the backup workspace. Back up your current workspace before replacement. Invalid or incomplete archives are rejected.

Records, currency and deleted vehicles

Choose your workspace currency before adding records. Amounts are not automatically converted when a language or display preference changes. A quote, an incurred bill and its payment are different records; check which one you need before entering an amount.

Deleted vehicles can be restored from the deleted-records list in Settings. Deletion and archiving do not reset the Free allowance. Sample data is separate; return to your own vehicles before backing up or restoring real data.

Do not uninstall the app as an initial troubleshooting step: the developer cannot recover a local workspace that has no backup.

Reports and photos

Review report mode and selected vehicles before exporting. Owner reports may include costs and results that are not appropriate for a buyer. A report exported or shared outside the app is a separate copy; later edits do not update it.

For batch reports, Preview opens the generated pages in iOS’s print interface. You can close it without printing and then export the same prepared report. Batch and branding features require Pro for real data.

When adding an image, select a photo or request the camera. If camera access was denied, enable it for this app in iOS Settings. Choosing an image does not automatically send it to support or an online service.

Appearance, language and diagnostics

Settings is available from the main tabs. Choose Light, Dark or System appearance, your supported interface language and distance units there. English US is the default interface language.

If an optional analytics and diagnostics switch is present, it starts off and can be changed in Settings. Core local records do not require opting in. See the Privacy Policy for the information used by this option.

TestFlight feedback

For an internal beta, use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight or email support. Include the exact build number and whether the issue involves onboarding, a vehicle record, a report or a purchase. TestFlight purchase behavior uses Apple’s test environment and should not be confused with a normal App Store subscription. Support will never ask you to send an Apple password or verification code.
