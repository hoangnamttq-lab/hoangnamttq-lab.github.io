Who provides this app

Learn Japanese: Kanji & JLPT is provided by Nam Tran. Contact: hoangnamttq@gmail.com. This policy applies to the iOS app and its widget, and describes this version’s actual features.

Learning data on your device

Lesson answers, progress, review schedules, study lists, saved words, preferences and paused sessions are stored in the app’s local storage. No app account is required. The app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking SDK, AI service or developer-operated backend. The developer does not automatically receive your learning data. iOS device backup and device-transfer settings may include app data under Apple’s control.

Widgets and shared storage

The app and its widget share a small App Group snapshot containing selected study content, review due dates, display preferences and the last verified subscription horizon. Widget answer-reveal state and deliberate review actions can be stored locally in an event queue. The app applies review events once when it is opened. Displaying a widget or simply opening the app does not record learning credit.

Purchases and connectivity

Apple processes purchases, payment details, receipts and refunds under its own policies. The app uses StoreKit to verify subscription transactions and access rights on the device; it does not receive your full payment card number. It observes whether a network route is available to retry verification when connectivity returns. It does not send network status or learning records to a developer server. Apple can receive transaction and diagnostic data through its services.

Speech and reminders

Japanese pronunciation uses Apple’s system speech synthesis. Available voices and any downloads are managed by iOS. No microphone recording or speech recognition is used. Daily reminders and the optional 10-second test reminder are scheduled locally only after you request them and allow notifications. Reminder intent, local time and sound are saved on the device. The app reads its own pending requests and notification entries still present in Notification Center to show diagnostics. A delivered entry does not mean you read it. Turning off the daily reminder cancels that daily request; the test is a separate, one-time notification. You can change permission in iOS Settings.

Ratings and sharing

The app keeps completion dates and review-request timestamps locally to limit automatic App Store review requests. It does not read your star rating or know whether you submitted a review. Test/development request quotas are separate from production. Rate App opens Apple’s review page after release; beta builds explain the availability limit. Share App opens the iOS share sheet only when you choose to share. The app does not know the recipient or destination unless the system explicitly supplies that information, and it does not automatically send a message.

Export and support

Export is user initiated through the system share sheet. You choose the destination and are responsible for reviewing exported content. Contacting support opens your mail app or lets you copy the address; the app does not automatically attach or send progress. If you send an email or a file, the developer receives the information you choose to share and uses it to respond to and resolve that request. Ask by email to delete support correspondence; legally required records may need to be retained. No fixed retention period is promised.

Your choices

Subscription expiry, a refund or turning off renewal does not delete saved progress or lists. You can remove saved words or lists in the app and export progress. Uninstalling the app can remove local app data; removal of backups is controlled by iOS. The app does not offer server-side account deletion because it has no developer account system.

This website and policy updates

The pages contain no developer analytics or advertising scripts. The effective date identifies this policy version. Updated policies are published here and bundled with later app versions.
